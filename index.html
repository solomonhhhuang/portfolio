About
Me

Photographer focused on sports, people, travel, and everyday moments.

I’m Solomon Huang, a photographer inspired by film photography, newspaper layouts, scrapbook pages, and the small details that make an image feel personal.

I like taking photos that feel natural, cinematic, and a little nostalgic. Whether I’m shooting basketball, portraits, city streets, or travel memories, I try to capture real movement, emotion, and atmosphere.

This portfolio is a space for my favourite projects, from Montreal street photography to people, sports, and editorial-style work. I’m always looking to grow my style and tell better stories through images.
